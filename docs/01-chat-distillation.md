# Distillation of the original Gemini chat log (Sept 2025)

Source: `autismtheorychats.txt` and `### Recodified Theory AI-Assisted S.txt`. Names reduced to initials.

**Context.** Casey (audio specialist / event tech; camp counselor for developmentally disabled children 1989–1998; group-home work with disabled adults) developed these ideas under a standing directive: *facilitate communication skills in high-functioning autistic people*, later widened to the severely affected ("severity-first: if we can help them we can adjust for people less severe").

## 1. Product / feature ideas that came out of the chat

1. **Per-person programmed AI.** "An AI could be programmed specific to each person… with the right directives an AI would be able to make connections that a human couldn't." Camp orientation day — going through each kid's personality and trigger words — is the manual analog.
2. **Conduit, not therapist.** The AI is a communication conduit; a human tunes it for the client through back-and-forth. Later refined to *light human-in-the-loop*: a therapist keeps the AI aligned and course-corrected.
3. **Schedule-change handling.** First chosen pain point. On a change, present a new map / new "why" / new "next", not just "the plan changed."
4. **Non-text, multi-sensory signaling for non-readers.** Auditory motifs, green/yellow/red light grammar, tactile cues (warmth/vibration), later olfactory and rhythmic cues.
5. **Passive, in-situ training.** No sit-down training. Log baseline reactions, pair new cues with already-predictable events, human verbally reinforces ("Here comes the dinner sound, A.").
6. **Proactive environmental orchestrator.** A constant low-level predictive "base track" plus a reactive modulator — framed as a live sound mix.
7. **Vocalization pattern replacement.** Learn the signature (frequency, duration, context) of screeches/growls and offer an alternative that serves the *same function* — not a predetermined calming one. ("What if the noise they need is not soothing at all?")
8. **Temporal anchor.** A perfectly reliable external timekeeper replacing the human who walked A. through his calendar board. Environmental anchors (light/hum before meals), emotional anchors (stress detection → consistent response), narrative anchors ("The moon is now in the sky… lights off in five minutes").
9. **Sentinel for disruption.** Fire alarm / power outage triggers a distinct, pre-learned "major unexpected change" pattern.
10. **Gating mechanism** (audio-compressor analogy) — detect the earliest stage of the fear-of-change feedback loop and gate it.
11. **Non-invasive "neural prosthetic."** Read externalized output (vocalizations, movement) with consumer sensors, not an implant.
12. **Cheap, open, off-the-shelf.** Smart lights/speakers, vibrating cushions, weighted items. "As inexpensive as collaboratively possible and preferably free."
13. **ASLT protocol** (AI-Assisted Savant Language Translation): pattern recognition → contextual analysis → translation/generation, with a therapist validating at each phase.
14. **Calendar-savant reverse engineering.** Map date→weekday outputs to reconstruct and explain the internal algorithm.
15. **Proof-of-concept path.** Digital twin on synthetic/anonymized audio → validation by practitioners (SLPs, special-ed staff) → credentialed research collaborator for an ethical study.
16. **Rejected:** screen-based/Roblox interface layer (would feed game addiction).

## 2. People (design inputs)

- **A.** Severe need for structure; self-harm and aggression when the plan broke; **couldn't read, couldn't sit still.** Trigger: meals were called with no set interval before eating — if late he'd unravel. Improved briefly when staff walked him through a **calendar/event board** as each thing happened, but 24/7 coverage wasn't sustainable. Sent home. "I thought I could help him but needed more time."
- **M.** Communicated through song; upset when *unchosen* counselors sang to her — a selective, ritualized musical back-and-forth.
- **S.** Non-verbal but understood conversation; deep connection with Casey — grounds for presuming competence regardless of verbal output.
- **Mx.** Memorized the atlas; communicated through route numbers and geography.
- **B.** Calendar calculation.
- **F.** Madonna obsession; sang and dressed as her; shy, high-pitched voice; drew.
- **J.** Strong frustration reactions; distinct smell (a non-verbal channel).
- **Br.** Exaggerated eccentricities when uncomfortable.
- **E.** Fear of balloons.
- **Child (ADHD).** Random screams/noises — hypothesis: the noise keeps a needed pattern going.
- **Child (gamer).** Meltdowns over Roblox inconsistency; goal is self-regulation, not removal.
- **Adult (neurodivergent).** Brilliant, "feature not a bug."

## 3. Theoretical claims

- **Talking to an LLM ≈ talking to an autistic savant.** Need for precision, delimiters, specialized vocabularies, non-verbal comprehension. Justifies using prompt-engineering craft as the method for building per-person communication models.
- **Persona theory.** Conflicting directives cause "buggy" behavior; people are a mix of contradictory personalities; the next step is reconciling contradictions rather than avoiding them.
- **Personal predictive model.** Schedule = model of reality; a change = catastrophic model failure; effects compound. (Maps to predictive-coding / insistence-on-sameness literature.)
- **Scaling / lock-in hypothesis.** Fear of change scales; the more fear, the more "locked in."
- **Noise as homeostatic alignment.** Random vocalizations impose a pattern; games are self-administered predictability machines.
- **Feature not a bug.** If sensory/predictability needs are met, capacity is freed for higher-level thinking. "My brain has absolutely been unlocked by AI."
- **Savant language as structured language.** Idiosyncratic communication has learnable syntax/grammar.

## 4. Constraints and values

- Conduit, not therapist; assist, don't diagnose or cure.
- Cheap/free, open, collaborative; no new screen addiction; real-world alignment.
- Research ethics: synthetic data first, practitioner validation, then credentialed collaborator.
- Respect for labels and privacy.
- Caution: removing a root cause could help *or* destabilize; behaviors are unpredictable.
- Openness over ownership ("I would sacrifice every single dime if that could be my legacy").
